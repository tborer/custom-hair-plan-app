## Publishing Best Practices

### Timing Optimization
- **Blog posts:** Publish Tuesday-Thursday at 9am EST for best SEO crawl impact
- **Social pins:** Pinterest performs well Thursday-Saturday
- **Email newsletters:** Send Wednesday-Friday morning when open rates are highest
- **Instagram/Reels:** Monday-Wednesday evening during scroll time

### Quality Control Checklist Before Publishing
- [ ] Content passes keyword research validation
- [ ] Internal linking structure implemented
- [ ] Schema markup validated in testing tool
- [ ] Mobile preview shows no rendering issues
- [ ] Readability score > 60 (Flesch-Kincaid)
- [ ] Grammar/spelling verified
- [ ] Images have alt text and filenames optimized
