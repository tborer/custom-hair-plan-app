# Custom Hair Plan App - Copywriting & Conversion Guide

## Core Messaging Framework

### Primary Value Proposition (Hero)
```
A personalized plan to regrow and keep your hair

We assess stress, nutrition, thinning patterns, and pair proven supplements with topicals. 
Get a unique free insight, then unlock your full plan.
```

**Why this works:**
- "Personalized" → high perceived value
- "Regrow AND keep" → addresses both loss and retention pain points
- Specific methodology (stress + nutrition + topicals) → credibility
- Free incentive (insight) reduces friction
- Clear CTA structure

### Secondary Propositions by Audience Segment

| Segment | Messaging Angle | Example Copy |
|---------|----------------|--------------|
| **Women 35-65** | Hormonal thinning, gentle options | "Address hormonal thinning without harsh chemicals. Gentle, effective solutions for her hair." |
| **Men with Male Pattern** | DHT blocker focus | "Block the hormone causing thinning. Science-backed minoxidil alternatives that work." |
| **Early Thinning Prevention** | Early intervention | "Notice increased shedding? Stop it before you see visible bald patches." |
| **Post-Chemical Damage** | Repair & regrow | "Rebuild your hair follicles after harsh treatments. Recovery, not just treatment." |

## Voice & Tone Guidelines

### Do:
✓ Write conversationally (you + I = we)
✓ Acknowledge hair loss is emotionally difficult without being melodramatic
✓ Explain science accessibly (define acronyms like DHT)
✓ Show empathy for frustration and embarrassment associated with thinning
✓ Use specific numbers ("6 months not 2 years") rather than vague claims

### Don't:
✗ Promise guaranteed results (use "helps," "supports," "promotes")
✗ Use fear tactics to scare users further
✗ Claim medical treatment without disclaimers
✗ Compare against competitors directly (compare vs. standard, not brand names)

### Sample "Empathetic but Professional" Voice Example
```
"We know finding effective hair regrowth solutions feels frustrating. 
Many people have wasted money on supplements that didn't work—often because 
what works depends on YOUR unique biology, not what's popular on Instagram.

That's why we don't sell generic packages. We assess your specific factors: 
stress levels, nutrition gaps, thinning patterns, and current medications. 

Your plan reflects those details. No one else will personalize for you like this.
```

## A/B Testing Copy Recommendations

### Test 1: Hero Headline Variations
| Variant | Angle | Expected CTR Impact |
|---------|-------|---------------------|
| "Regrow hair in 6 months..." | Time-bound promise | Baseline |
| "Your personalized plan, generated in minutes" | Emphasize personalization | +5-10% |
| "Stop wasting money on supplements that don't work for you" | Pain/frustration angle | +8-15% |
| "Science-backed hair regrowth (no gimmicks)" | Trust/credibility angle | +3-7% |

### Test 2: CTA Button Text
| Option | Wording | Expected Conversion |
|--------|---------|---------------------|
| Start free assessment | Direct, low-friction | Baseline |
| Unlock my personalized plan | Value-focused | +10-15% |
| Get your free insight now | Urgency-focused | +5-8% |
| See if this is for you | Curiosity-driven | -5% (lower CTR, higher qual leads) |

### Test 3: Pricing Page Urgency Triggers
| Option | Copy Element | Effectiveness Notes |
|--------|---------------|---------------------|
| "Limited to first 50 users" | Social proof scarcity | High impact for early adopters |
| "$19.99 ends in 24 hours" | False urgency (avoid) | Can backfire with repeat visitors |
| "Join 2,000+ satisfied customers" | Community validation | Steady conversion boost |

Run tests for minimum 5 days to account for day-of-week effects. Use 100+ conversions per variant before declaring winner statistically.

## Compliance & Legal Copy Requirements

### Required Disclaimers (Place in footer or near checkout)
```
"This content is educational and not a substitute for medical advice. 
Consult your clinician before starting any new supplement regimen or treatment.

Custom Hair Plan products are sold as dietary supplements, not drugs. 
Results vary by individual. We do not guarantee specific outcomes."
```

### GDPR/CCPA Cookie Notice Language
```
"We use cookies to improve your experience and personalize content. 
By using this site you agree to our cookie policy. You can adjust preferences 
in settings or choose to opt out of analytics tracking at any time via link."
```

### Payment Terms (Stripe Integration)
```
"Pricing shown is per plan subscription. Tax applied at checkout based on location. 
Subscription renews monthly unless cancelled. Full refund available within 30 days 
if plan does not meet stated expectations."
```
